ABOUT AJOU

A University Shaped by Innovation

Ajou University is a research-oriented university located in Suwon, South Korea. Since its foundation, Ajou has developed an academic environment focused on education, research, innovation and international engagement.

The university's academic environment spans engineering, information technology, natural sciences, business, humanities and other fields.

OUR STORY

From Engineering to a Global University

An overview of Ajou's development from its early engineering-focused beginnings.

1973

The Beginning

Ajou's predecessor, Ajou Engineering Junior College, was founded in March 1973. It began with an emphasis on engineering and technical education.

1981

University Development

Ajou was elevated to a comprehensive university, expanding its academic structure and developing new fields of study.

Today

Looking Forward

Ajou continues to develop education, research, international collaboration and interdisciplinary learning for a changing world.

LOCATION

Located in Suwon, South Korea

Ajou University's main campus is located in Suwon, approximately 30 kilometers south of Seoul. Its location provides access to a major metropolitan area while maintaining a dedicated university campus environment.
